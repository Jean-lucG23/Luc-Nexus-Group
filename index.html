Problems we help you solve

Most enquiries start with one of these. If yours isn’t listed, tell us about it.

Wi‑Fi that drops or crawls

Dead spots, congested channels and badly placed access points are common, fixable causes.

Slow internet at the office

We check whether the bottleneck is the line, the router, the cabling or the way the network is set up.

Choppy video calls

Latency, jitter and uneven bandwidth sharing all hurt calls. We find which one is to blame.

An unsecured network

Default passwords, flat networks and missing firewall rules leave doors open. We close them.

Moving to the cloud

Connecting offices and staff to cloud services reliably and securely, including hybrid setups.

Outages and load-shedding

Plan power backup for routers and key switches so a power cut does not take you offline.
